That brings me to part two, the app. What you see is always the network’s prediction. The case sits in the middle, and each screen is wired to its part: the CPU, the graphics card, the fans. I can change the room temperature and the load, drag the fan curves like in ASUS Fan Xpert, or click any fan to flip it or switch it off. The network answers in a tenth of a second.
