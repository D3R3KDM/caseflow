Step four: training. The simulator made its own training data: 150 layouts, about four hours of GPU time. Sixteen layouts were kept out as a test the network never sees. The loss compares the air and the heat in every cell, plus the air entering the two coolers, because that sets the part temperatures. 300 epochs took 35 minutes. On the right, the test error stops improving after about 60 epochs: more data would help more than more training.
