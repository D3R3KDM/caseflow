Only if asked: the solver. Each cell keeps 19 particle populations. Every step, they collide, relaxing toward equilibrium with a Smagorinsky turbulence model, and then stream to the neighboring cells. Walls bounce them back, fan cells have a set air velocity, and the layer around the case is open to the room. Each fan runs at its operating point against the case pressure. Checks: 99.9 percent of the heat leaves through the openings, and the temperatures converge to a tenth of a degree.
