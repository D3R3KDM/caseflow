Step three is the neural network, a 3D U-Net. The input is the case itself: eight numbers per cell, like solid or air, fan cells and their air velocity, and the CPU and GPU heat zones. Four times, the network halves the grid, down to 5 by 3 by 5 cells, so its deepest features see the whole case: a fan on the top changes the flow at the front. Then it scales back up, and skip connections bring back the 6-millimeter detail. The output is the 3D airflow and the heat per watt, in exactly the format of a simulation. 13.5 million weights, and one prediction takes a tenth of a second.
