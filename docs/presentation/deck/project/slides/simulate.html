Step one: simulate one layout. The case becomes a grid of 80 by 40 by 80 cells, each 6 millimeters. My lattice Boltzmann solver computes the airflow at three fan speeds at once on the GPU. Then the heat: how much warmer the air gets for every watt of CPU or GPU power. And the dust: forty thousand particles that drift with the air and stick where they land. All of that takes about 90 seconds. On the right: my layout, and the worst one.
