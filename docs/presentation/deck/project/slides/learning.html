Two more checks. Trained on a quarter, a half, and all of the layouts, the error at the cooler intakes falls from 20 to 16 to 10 percent. So more simulated layouts are the direct way to a better network. And dust: the network predicts no dust, but the simulator’s particles in the network’s air land within two percentage points of the simulation.
