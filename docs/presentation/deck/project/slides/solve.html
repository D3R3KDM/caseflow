Step two makes it interactive. Heat is linear in power, so I store the temperature rise per watt, at three fan speeds. But the fans follow their curves, and their speed depends on the temperature. So the browser solves for the point where both agree. Here that is 65.6 degrees; my real PC measured 65.3. It takes about a millisecond, and it stays within half a degree of a full re-simulation.
