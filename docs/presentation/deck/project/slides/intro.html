First, the background. A gaming PC is a box of heat: my graphics card and CPU can turn more than 400 watts into heat. Fans carry it out, but where they are mounted decides where the air goes and how hot the parts get. My case has eight fan mounts: four stock fans, and three spare fans of my own. So I built three things: a GPU airflow simulator of my exact case, a 3D neural network that learns from it, and a web app that shows both in 3D. The talk has five parts.
